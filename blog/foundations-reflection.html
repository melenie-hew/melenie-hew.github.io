Foundations Reflection

What are three new things you have learnt about yourself and your ego due to the Human Skills learning?

I have learnt that my perception of intelligence was not accurate. I used to think that if you found something was very hard or very challenging, that maybe you did not have the right skills to complete it or that you weren’t intelligent in that sense.

I also realized that the growth mindset does actually work. Initially I resisted trying out the methods to foster a growth mindset because I wasn’t completely convinced that it would work. But in the last few sprints, I have exercised more empathy and tried to foster a mindset of ‘not yet’ and it has helped me mentally get over some barriers.

Aside from the above, I have also been practising conscious listening more and more in my day-to-day life. I have found this really helps with the quality of the conversation and also I feel like I am able to engage more easily with the person I am speaking with.

What are the role of values, empathy, and self-awareness in learning and programming?

Only when we reflect on what our values are, will we be able to figure out what is important to us and also what makes me who we are today. This is important because in learning and programming, you will encounter times when you feel like the challenge you face is too big, and its in these moments where you have to remind yourself of what matters to you and why you got into this in the first place.

Empathy and self-awareness come hand-in-hand when it comes to programming and learning. By being aware of our limitations and exercising empathy, we can lower our ego, and ask for help with the challenges we face.

What has surprised you the most about the Human Skills learning?

For sprint 3 and 4, as we were being introduced to javascript, I quickly got overwhelmed with my lack of knowledge. However, I tried to foster a growth mindset by telling myself that I may not get the concepts by the end of the sprint, but that I would get it eventually.

What were the most challenging aspects of the Human Skills learning?

Actually wanting to do them. I felt so much like just jumping to the tech parts for most of the sprints but then I have come to realize just how important the human skills learning is. It allows me to almost ‘check in’ with myself and reflect on how I’m tracking along.

Why do you think we, a programming school, are spending so much time focusing on Human Skills learning in a web development Bootcamp course?

Because I believe that is what makes a good employee. It helps develop well-rounded and grounded individuals.

Does the time you spent studying Human Skills learning here feel like a waste of time? Should you have just used that time to practise programming instead? Justify your answer.

I do believe that there has been some great benefit to the human skills learning. While the programming bit of the programme has been hard, the mental challenges to get over those hurdles have been even harder.
