Wall Activities
Taha Tinana (Physical Wellbeing) Keep up my training sessions with my personal trainer and endeavour to go to the gym at least two other times a week
Taha Whānau (Family Wellbeing) Spend time with my family during meal times and spend some time doing family activities on my days off
Taha Hinengaro (Emotional and Mental Wellbeing) Practice mindfulness and just sitting in silence for a few minutes each morning before starting any work or study
Taha Wairua (Spiritual Wellbeing) Praying each day before I head to bed
Whenua (Interconnections to the land and environment) Sit in the sun for a few minutes each day
